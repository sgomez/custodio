- §18 step 3 and step 5 say to register the module with `modutil -add` as in §6. On Fedora that fails: `ERROR: Failed to add module

  has already initialized the module (see F45.md). Nothing is lost: the tokens reach both clients through the proxy. Without `</dev/null` the command waits at its p11-kit warning
  prompt even with `-force`.
