# F176 — Sign through the socket: polkit on the peer's pidfd, and the journal fields

## Scope
Builds, on the connection F175 already admits: (1) `Sign` on the p2p interface, same name, path and
arguments as the bus method; the key is looked up by the peer's kernel uid; (2) one polkit
`CheckAuthorization` per call for `com.rfirma.custodio.sign` with `AllowUserInteraction` on a
`unix-process` subject of `{pidfd, uid}` (the connection's `SO_PEERPIDFD` and the peer's `SO_PEERCRED`
uid; nothing the peer sends); (3) the journal fields `docs/audit.md` documents for the transport and the
peer's label; (4) the comment in `data/com.rfirma.custodio.policy` on the `owner` annotation and the
tests that fail without the annotation.

Fixed by earlier work, not re-decided (`docs/findings/F170.md` Decisions, `docs/decisions.md`): the uid
comes from the kernel only; 65534 and unreadable uids never get here (F175 refuses them before any
method); polkit is asked with `{pidfd, uid}` under the `owner` annotation `unix-user:custodio`; where
polkit lacks the subject (older than 124, or any error reply) the service fails closed.

Behaviour fixed here:
- A missing key or another uid's key is `NoSuchKey`, no polkit call, no entry (as on the bus).
- Denial gives `PolkitDenied`; any error from polkit (unreachable, a reply that is not understood,
  "Error parsing unix-process subject", a refusal of the subject) gives `AuthorizationUnavailable`; a
  peer whose pidfd shows it has exited gives `AuthorizationUnavailable` too (the service checks the pidfd
  itself, fail-closed, and polkit's own error for a gone process is mapped the same way). There is no
  retry with another subject kind (`system-bus-name`, pid plus start time) and no downgrade. Nothing is
  cached: two calls on one connection ask polkit twice.
- Journal: every entry of a request (sign, import, delete, replace) gets a transport field (`bus` or
  `socket`); a socket entry also gets the peer's label, which is only ever the string that already passed
  `peer_label::check` (so no newline or control byte can reach the journal). `CUSTODIO_UID` and
  `CUSTODIO_PID` of a socket entry are the peer's kernel uid and pid (the pid from `SO_PEERCRED`).
  `purge` entries are written at start-up, are not requests and get no transport field (documented).
  Bus entries differ from today's only by the transport field. The field names are the builder's choice,
  documented in `docs/audit.md` under its compatibility rule (added only, none renamed) and in the table of
  fields, with the `MESSAGE` shapes unchanged. The audit failure path is the bus's: an entry that cannot be
  submitted gives `AuditUnavailable` and no signature.
- The comment on `org.freedesktop.policykit.owner` in the policy file is documentation: it is not tested
  by its wording. What is tested is the annotation itself, through behaviour (Evidence).

Left for others: every other socket method being unknown, introspection of exactly two methods, bounds,
rate-limited refusal lines and `docs/api.md` (F177); the real polkit and real confinement (the VM cases,
case 17, and the e2e of a later checkpoint; the claim in F170 that `identity` keeps the owner match is
**inferred** there and is proven at the checkpoint, not here). The audit entry for a refused connection
stays the stderr line of F175.

Environment checked: `dbus-daemon` is on PATH (`tests/polkit.rs` already starts a private one with a fake
`org.freedesktop.PolicyKit1` authority); the peer's pidfd of a real child is obtainable without privilege.

Cost of the new tests:
- `crates/custodiod/tests/snap_socket_sign.rs`: in-process; one private `dbus-daemon` with the scripted
  polkit per case (about 50 ms to start, as in `tests/polkit.rs`), `UnixStream` pairs, the F175 `test-seam`
  source, and real child processes only for the exited-peer and real-pid cases. About 14 cases, estimated
  under 6 s total, parallel with other integration tests. Waits are on the reply or end of file, none on a
  fixed `sleep`; the exited peer waits on the pidfd becoming readable.
- Unit tests of the new field functions in `audit`: milliseconds.
- No new script test, no e2e case: real polkit and journald on the socket are the checkpoint's.
- `just mutants-diff F176`: the diff is the socket sign path plus the audit fields; estimated like F175's
  16 + 5 mutants plus the audit additions, about 5 to 10 minutes, well inside one command.
Fits in about six steps, one to two sessions; no split.

## Evidence
- Sign on the socket signs the peer's own key → `snap_socket_sign::signs_own_key` → the scripted polkit
  authorizes; the signature verifies against the key's certificate for RSA 2048 and P-256 (one case per
  type); the reply is on the socket, with the bus-method arguments.
- Key found by the peer's uid only → `::other_uid_key_is_no_such_key` → peer A asks for peer B's id, and
  for a missing id: `NoSuchKey` for both, scripted polkit saw zero calls, journal has no entry.
- One polkit call of the right shape → `::asks_polkit_once_on_the_pidfd_subject` → exactly one
  `CheckAuthorization`: action `com.rfirma.custodio.sign`, flags `AllowUserInteraction`, subject kind
  `unix-process` with `pidfd` and `uid` set to the kernel's uid for that connection (a seam uid, not the
  service's own; the pidfd is the one the seam gave, checked by the subject's pidfd referring to the same
  process as a real child's pidfd in the real-pid case) and no `pid`/`start-time` downgrade.
  Fails if the subject is `system-bus-name`, the uid is the service's own, or two calls are made.
- The uid a peer claims is never used → `::claimed_uid_is_ignored` → a client that sends `AUTH EXTERNAL`
  with another uid is rejected (as F175), and an `ANONYMOUS` client with a body or argument naming another
  uid still gets the kernel uid's keys and subject.
- Denial → `::denial_is_polkit_denied` → `PolkitDenied`; nothing signed; journal outcome `denied`.
- polkit unreachable or erroring → `::polkit_errors_are_authorization_unavailable` → one case each for no
  authority on the bus, an error reply, and an unparsable reply: `AuthorizationUnavailable`; nothing signed;
  outcome `authorization_unavailable`.
- polkit without the subject fails closed → `::old_polkit_fails_closed` → the scripted polkit answers
  "Error parsing unix-process subject" / InvalidArgs to the pidfd subject: `AuthorizationUnavailable`, the
  scripted polkit saw exactly that one call (no retry with another subject), nothing signed.
- Exited peer → `::exited_peer_is_authorization_unavailable` → a real child's pidfd taken as the peer's,
  the child killed and waited for; the polkit scripted to authorize: `AuthorizationUnavailable`, nothing
  signed, entry `authorization_unavailable`. Negative control in the same file: the same scripted polkit
  with the child alive signs.
- Nothing cached → `::every_call_asks_polkit` → authorize, then deny, then authorize on one connection:
  the replies follow the script call by call and polkit saw three calls.
- The `owner` annotation is needed → `::scripted_polkit_requires_the_owner_annotation` → the scripted
  polkit reads the shipped `data/com.rfirma.custodio.policy` and, like polkit 127 in F170's table, answers
  "Only trusted callers" for a subject uid other than the caller's unless the action has the `owner`
  annotation for the caller: with the shipped file the call signs; with the annotation removed from a
  copy of the file the same call is `AuthorizationUnavailable`. Together with the existing
  `tests/policy.rs` owner check (and its negative control, unchanged), this is the test that fails without
  the annotation.
- Journal fields on the socket → `::socket_entries_carry_transport_label_and_peer` → for signed, denied,
  unavailable and signing-failed (an unusable record) calls: transport `socket`, the peer's label as the
  seam gave it (`snap.firefox.firefox (enforce)`), `CUSTODIO_UID` the seam uid, `CUSTODIO_PID` the peer's
  pid (a real child's pid in the real-pid case, from `SO_PEERCRED`; not the service's pid), the app-id
  state recorded as before.
- Bus entries unchanged but for the transport → `audit` unit tests and the existing `tests/audit.rs`,
  `app_id_audit`, `import_audit`, `delete_audit`, `replace_audit`, `kind_audit` suites → the same
  expectations as before with exactly one new field, `bus`, on each request entry; a purge entry has none.
  A unit test compares `fields()` of an entry with the field removed against the previous exact list.
- A label that is not valid can never reach the journal → `audit` unit test → the label setter takes only
  a value that passed `peer_label::check` (type-level or checked): an entry built with a label holding a
  newline or NUL is refused; the check function is the F174 one.
- The fields are documented → the existing `audit/docs_tests.rs` mechanism, extended → every field name the
  code can emit appears in the `docs/audit.md` table and every documented field is emitted (tested
  against the code, not by wording), and a negative control for an undocumented field.
- Audit failure → `::audit_failure_is_audit_unavailable` → a failing sink gives `AuditUnavailable` with
  no signature on the socket, as on the bus (`FailingSink`).
- Secrets stay out → `::no_secret_in_entries_or_errors` → the key bytes, the signed data and the
  signature appear in no journal field, no error text and no `Debug` of the new types (the sentinel-key
  fixtures `rsa2048-sentinel.p12`/`p256-sentinel.p12` as in `no_plaintext`).
- Policy comment → no test of its wording; reviewed by the evaluator. The annotation is proven above.
- Gate checks → `just quick`; `just mutants-diff F176` → clean.
