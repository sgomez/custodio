# F137 — End-to-end cases: keys survive a PCR change and restarts

## Scope
New cases `pcr-*` for SPEC acceptance 5, first part ("after extending PCR 7 and restarting both `swtpm` and the service, every key still
signs"; the upgrade half is another feature), run against the real service in the debian13 container. Alice imports an RSA 2048 and a P-256
key (kind `tpm` in `swtpm` and in `lacking`). The guest, as root with `tpm2-tools` on the service's own `swtpm`, then:
1. reads PCRs 0, 7 and 11 (sha256) and signs once with each key (RSA PKCS#1 and ECDSA, verified with `openssl` against the served certificate,
   as `sign.py` does);
2. extends PCR 7 and the other two with `tpm2_pcrextend` and reads them again (they differ); signs again with each key;
3. restarts the TPM (`swtpm` stopped over its control socket and started on the same state directory, so the seeds persist and the PCRs are
   back at their reset values; in `lacking` the real `swtpm` behind the filter is the one restarted, and the filter too if it cannot reconnect:
   builder's choice) and then the service (`systemctl restart custodio`, the order of F129), waits on the service's `registered`/ready
   condition and on a new main pid, and checks the keys are still listed with the same ids and kind; signs again with each key.
The signing goes through the module and polkit as in `sign-*`; no authorization is cached. The ids and the signing helpers are `sign.py`'s
where they fit; the judging is python3 `-I` functions of files with a self-test, like `noplain.py`.
- **Already proven elsewhere, not repeated.** The same property through the bus with a fake polkit, `swtpm`, PCR extensions in several banks,
  the restart order, and the cleared TPM: F129 (`tpm_lifecycle`, part of `just quick`). The kinds and every mechanism and PSS parameter: F133/F134.
  Here only the real daemon, module, polkit, `systemctl restart` and the installed package are added. The upgrade from one M4 build to the next
  (second half of acceptance 5), reboot, package gates and `just e2e` as a whole are proven at the checkpoint or by their own features.
- **Modes.** `swtpm` and `lacking`: both keys are `tpm`; the cases run, and the `ListKeys` kind must be `tpm` before and after (a wrong kind
  fails, the run is not vacuous). `none`: no TPM, the keys are `encrypted-host`, there is no PCR to extend and no `swtpm` to restart; the cases
  are reported skipped (`skip.list` with a reason). **"TPM-bound" keys** (`encrypted-tpm`) cannot be created in the container (deviation `F134` in
  `SPEC_DEVIATIONS.md`): this feature extends that entry to F137 in its own commit and amendment, with the same coverage statement (F129 imports
  `encrypted-tpm` keys through a credential double and checks they sign after the PCR extension and both restarts; the real `systemd-creds` is
  not run for it). No `encrypted-tpm` claim is made by these cases.
- **Cases** (each reported exactly once; the builder may add one; every id matches `pcr-*`): `pcr-extended-signs` (PCR 7, 0 and 11 changed
  in the TPM, then each key signs and the signature verifies), `pcr-restarted-signs` (the TPM and the service were really restarted: new service
  pid, the PCRs read back at their reset values, different from the extended ones; each key signs and verifies; the keys are listed with the same
  ids and kind), `pcr-signs-every-key` (the enumeration is of every key the service lists with kind `tpm`, not two chosen ones: each signs
  after the restarts; fails with fewer than the two imported). Signing refusals, a wrong kind or a missing key fail the case.
- **Placement.** Ids in `e2e/targets/debian13/cases.list` (their outcome depends on the TPM), not in `e2e/cases.list`; `none` skips in
  `e2e/targets/debian13/skip.list`; `check-cases.sh` rejects a `pcr-*` skip in `swtpm` and `lacking` (the F133/F136 pattern). `select.sh` gets a
  group `pcr`, run after `tpmdup`, with keys of its own, deleted at the end. It is the **first step that restarts the service and the TPM in the
  middle of the main run**: the step must leave the service active with a registered agent, the TPM at the same socket with no extra process,
  and the counts and journal entries that later steps (upgrade, lifecycle, refusal) read as before. The builder checks that by reading those
  steps; the whole run is a checkpoint, but any later step that reads the main pid or a journal count is named in the finding.
- **Negative controls.**
  - Guest: a key made by `tpm2_create` whose policy is `PolicyPCR` on PCR 7 as read before the extension satisfies its policy session before the
    extension and fails it (policy failure) after, in the same run, so the extension demonstrably breaks a PCR-bound object and the signing
    that survives is the keys' property (SPEC: "no PCR policy"), not an extension that did nothing. Both halves must hold or the case fails.
  - Host (`scripts/test-e2e-pcr.sh`, no container, in the justfile script-test list): the judgements reject PCRs that did not change after the
    extension, PCRs that were not reset after the TPM restart, an unchanged service pid, a signature that does not verify, a signing refusal, a
    kind that changed, a missing key, fewer than two `tpm` keys; they accept the matching good evidence. `check-cases.sh` accepts the `none`
    skip and rejects a `pcr-*` skip in `swtpm` and `lacking`; the ids are in the debian13 list and not in `e2e/cases.list`.
- **Environment.** `tpm2_pcrextend`, `tpm2_pcrread`, `tpm2_createpolicy`/`tpm2_policypcr`, `swtpm_ioctl` and `systemctl` are in the debian13
  image or systemd (the `tpm2-tools` set is the one F136 used). Step 1 shows with one `just e2e-cases debian13 'pcr-*'` run that root in the guest
  can extend PCRs on the service's socket (`swtpm:path=/tmp/custodio-e2e-tpm/swtpm.sock`, also the filter's in `lacking`), that the PCRs are reset
  by the restart (`startup-clear`, `tpm-lib.sh`), that `systemctl restart custodio` works from the guest script and that the module signs
  afterwards; it records the commands and results in `docs/findings/F137.md` (short). `swtpm_setup`/`swtpm` do not run in the dev container
  (F136's finding), so this probe is the guest run itself. If the restart of the TPM or the service cannot be driven there, the cases are
  rewritten before anything else is built (a person's run or a deviation, with the command and its result in the finding).
- **Cost.** Host test `scripts/test-e2e-pcr.sh`: under 5 s, runs in parallel with the other script tests (default budget 60 s). Guest: two
  imports as alice (2–4 s each, as F134/F136), per key three signing rounds (RSA PKCS and ECDSA, about 1 s each with the polkit round trip,
  verification by `openssl`), a PCR read and extend (~0.5 s), a TPM restart (~1–2 s, the setup and wait on the sockets), a service restart and
  wait (~2–3 s), the control (~1 s); estimated 15–25 s per mode on top of shared setup, no new container. The two keys run as background jobs
  joined with `wait` (own context files), the control runs while the keys are being imported; the TPM is driven by one job at a time. Waits are
  on sockets, the pid change and the agent's `registered` line, never a fixed `sleep`. Each `just e2e-cases debian13 'pcr-*'` is estimated
  1–2 min (F135/F136 whole runs were 42–72 s); the verify's runs are `swtpm` and `lacking`, `none` is the builder's separate command. The full
  debian13 `swtpm` run (budget 430 s, measured 360 s, plus F135's ~20 s and F136's ~22 s) grows by another ~15–25 s; since that is near
  the budget the builder notes the sum in PROGRESS.md and, if the measured full run passes it, asks for more in BLOCKED.md. No `budgets.tsv`
  change expected here.
- Size: five steps, one session each at most.

## Evidence
- PCR 7 and others extended, then every key signs → `pcr-extended-signs` (`just e2e-cases debian13 'pcr-*'` in `swtpm`, and `E2E_TPM=lacking`) →
  `tpm2_pcrread` shows PCR 7, 0 and 11 different from the first reading; the RSA and the P-256 key each sign and `openssl` verifies the signatures.
- `swtpm` and the service restarted, every key still signs → `pcr-restarted-signs` (same runs) → the service's main pid is new, the PCRs
  are at reset values (not the extended ones), the keys are listed with the same ids and kind `tpm`, and both sign with signatures that verify.
- Every key, TPM and TPM-bound → `pcr-signs-every-key` (same runs) → each key of kind `tpm` that `ListKeys` shows signs, at least the two
  imported; `encrypted-tpm` keys → F129 (`tpm_lifecycle`) and the extended deviation entry, not run here.
- The extension has an effect, so surviving it means something → guest control in the cases → a PCR-7-policy object satisfies its policy session
  before the extension and fails (policy failure) after; if either half fails the case fails.
- Each judgement can fail → `scripts/test-e2e-pcr.sh` (host) → rejects each bad evidence listed above and accepts the good.
- Skipped where it does not apply → `E2E_TPM=none just e2e-cases debian13 'pcr-*'` → 3 cases reported skipped and accounted for with the
  `skip.list` reasons; host test: `check-cases.sh` accepts the `none` skip and rejects a `pcr-*` skip in `swtpm` and `lacking`; ids are in the
  debian13 list and not in `e2e/cases.list`.
- State left as found, nothing left running → the step's end → both keys absent from `ListKeys`, the service active with one new pid, one
  `swtpm` (and the filter in `lacking`) running, later steps in the same run still pass (full run at the checkpoint).
- Through the bus with a fake polkit, several banks, cleared TPM → F129, not repeated; upgrade half of acceptance 5 → its own feature.
- `just quick` green and the `verify` command → exit 0.
